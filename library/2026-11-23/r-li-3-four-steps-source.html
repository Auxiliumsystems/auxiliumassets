Gym floor sales

Every objection gets the same four steps.

“Too expensive.” “No time.” “I’ll start in January.” One answer shape for all of them.

Swipe →
Step 1 of 4
1

What they think.

Say it back to them, fairly. They should hear their own reason, not a sales line.

Step 2 of 4
2

Why it’s off.

One sentence. No lecture.

Step 3 of 4
3

What’s true instead.

The thing they haven’t weighed yet.

Step 4 of 4
4

Proof.

A real member, a real result. Then loop it until there’s nothing left to answer.

Worked example

“I’ll start in January.”

They think: “You want a clean start, fair enough.”
Why it’s off: “January is when the floor is busiest and the coaches are stretched.”
What’s true: “Start now and you walk into January already in a routine.”
Proof: someone you know who started in December and stuck with it.
