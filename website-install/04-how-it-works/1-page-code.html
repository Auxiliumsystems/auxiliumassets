How it works

From enquiry to a coach who's ready for them.

Here is exactly what happens when someone messages your gym, and what happens on your side to get it live.

For the member

What happens when someone enquires?

They get a reply in seconds and a booked visit in a few minutes. The assistant introduces itself as your gym's digital assistant, asks a few quick questions one at a time, offers available slots and books the visit. Your manager is notified, a coach is assigned, and reminders go out automatically.

  1. Someone enquires

    On your website, a social DM, WhatsApp or SMS, at any hour. The assistant replies in seconds, tells them it's an AI assistant, and uses your gym's voice.

  2. A few quick questions

    Goals, preferred times, experience, anything your sales team needs. One question at a time, friendly, never pushy. A human is always one message away.

  3. Visit booked

    The assistant books a tour, trial or consult into your calendar and sends reminders. Your manager is notified and assigns a coach.

  4. Coach walks in prepared

    The coach reads the answers before the visit. Once the person joins, their details are pushed into your membership system automatically.

For your team

What your coach sees before the visit.

Every booked visit comes with a short brief: what the person wants to achieve, when they can train, their experience, and anything they mentioned, like an injury or a wedding in June. Your coach walks in knowing who they're meeting.

  • Manager notified the moment a visit is booked
  • Answers to the qualifying questions attached to the booking
  • Joined members pushed into your membership system automatically
Example brief

Visit: Thursday 6:30pm

Goal: get stronger, lose 4–5 kg before summer
Training history: gym on and off, no coaching before
Best times: weekday evenings after 6
Notes: mentioned a dodgy left knee
Source: Instagram message, 22:47

Getting live

Done for you, set up in weeks.

  1. Walkthrough

    20 minutes. We look at where your enquiries come from today and what a reply in seconds would be worth.

  2. Onboarding questionnaire

    Your services, booking rules, qualifying questions and the assistant's name and voice.

  3. Build and train

    We connect your channels and calendar, train the assistant on your gym and test it end to end.

  4. Go live

    Your team is trained, you approve the conversations, and it starts answering. Typically three to six weeks from sign-up.

Questions about how it works

How fast does Auxilium reply to gym enquiries?
Within seconds, at any hour. At Space Gyms, half of all bookings in the first 30 days came in after staffed hours, when nobody would otherwise have replied until the next day.
Which channels does the assistant answer?
Your website chat, Instagram and Facebook messages, WhatsApp and SMS, plus calls you miss, which get an instant text back. Every conversation lands in one inbox your team can see and step into.
Can the assistant use my gym's name and personality?
Yes. Each gym names its own assistant and sets its tone. At Space Gyms she is called Aria. The assistant writes like your best front-desk person on a good day.
How long does setup take?
Typically three to six weeks from sign-up to live. We build it, train the assistant on your gym, connect your channels and calendar, and train your team. You answer an onboarding questionnaire and approve the assistant's voice.
