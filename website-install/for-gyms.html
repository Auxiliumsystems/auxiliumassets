Gym owners ask

Questions about Auxilium for gyms

Why does replying fast matter so much for gyms?
Because people shop around. Someone looking for a gym usually messages two or three at once, and the first to reply tends to win the visit. The MIT Lead Response Management Study found leads contacted within five minutes were 21 times more likely to qualify than those contacted after thirty minutes.
Does the assistant quote membership prices?
Not unless you want it to. By default it books the visit and mentions you have an offer on, so the price conversation happens in person with your team, where it converts best.
How long does setup take?
Typically three to six weeks from sign-up to live. We build it, train the assistant on your gym, connect your channels and calendar, and train your team. You answer an onboarding questionnaire and approve the assistant's voice.
What happens if the assistant doesn't know an answer?
It only answers from what it has been trained on for your gym. Anything else is handed to a member of your team, and the person is told someone will get back to them.
