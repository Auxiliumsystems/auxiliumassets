The problem

Enquiries land at 10pm. Your team replies at 10am.

Most gym enquiries arrive when the front desk is closed: late evenings, Sundays, the minute someone decides this is the week they start. By the time anyone replies the next morning, that person has messaged two other gyms and booked with whoever answered first.

It isn't a staffing failure. Nobody can be on the desk at 6am, on Instagram at 11pm and on the gym floor at the same time. Answering faster is worth more than buying more enquiries, and it's the part most gyms never fix.

How it works

From enquiry to a coach who's ready for them.

  1. Someone enquires

    On your website, a social DM, WhatsApp or SMS, at any hour. The assistant replies in seconds, tells them it's an AI assistant, and uses your gym's voice.

  2. A few quick questions

    Goals, preferred times, experience, anything your sales team needs. One question at a time, friendly, never pushy. A human is always one message away.

  3. Visit booked

    The assistant books a tour, trial or consult into your calendar and sends reminders. Your manager is notified and assigns a coach.

  4. Coach walks in prepared

    The coach reads the answers before the visit. Once the person joins, their details are pushed into your membership system automatically.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

What is Auxilium?
Auxilium is a done-for-you enquiry response service for gyms and leisure centres in Ireland. A digital assistant replies to every enquiry in seconds, 24 hours a day, asks a few qualifying questions, books a visit into your calendar and gives your coach the answers before the person arrives. Once they join, their details go into your existing membership system.
Is Auxilium a chatbot?
No. A chatbot answers questions; Auxilium is the front door for your gym's sales. It replies across your website, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and SMS, qualifies the person, books the visit, sends reminders, follows up with people who go quiet and asks happy members for Google reviews. It is set up and run for you rather than a tool you configure yourself.
Will people know they are talking to an AI?
Yes. Every Auxilium assistant says it is a digital assistant in its very first message, before it asks anything. That is what the EU AI Act's transparency rules (Article 50) require, and members don't mind: at one client gym, members name the assistant in their Google reviews and thank it alongside the team.
Do I need to change my membership software or CRM?
No. Auxilium works before someone becomes a member. When they join, their details are pushed into the system you already use, the same way a web form would. You keep your CRM, your billing and your access control.
